# For the Chief Executive Officer

**The question you are likely asking:** what is the state of our workforce, what needs my attention, and can we
deliver what we have promised with the people we have?

## What you get

| --- | --- | --- |

## How to read the numbers

- A figure that cannot be worked out shows as missing, never as zero. A trend with too little history says so.
- A forecast is a stated rule applied to stated inputs, not a model. Each result says what it rests on.
- Roll-ups name no one, and small groups are withheld. You will not get a list of individuals from the dashboard.
- Scores advise a person; they do not decide. Nothing in WPM ranks people for dismissal or pay.

## What it will not do

- It is not a payroll system of record, and payroll tax is a stub.
- It does not hold demographics. Equality monitoring exists but is **off** until a deployer records a lawful
  basis and defines the categories.
- The dashboard has not been reviewed by a person using assistive technology.

## Decisions that are yours

- Whether to take it past a demonstration. The [readiness assessment](../../../../spec/readiness-assessment.md) says **not ready**
  and lists the gates only people can close: an independent penetration test, an accessibility audit, the data
  protection impact assessment signed off by your officer, and a restore drill on your platform.
- Which equality impact assessments you approve before any scoring is used ([equality-impact/](../../../../spec/equality-impact/template.md)).
