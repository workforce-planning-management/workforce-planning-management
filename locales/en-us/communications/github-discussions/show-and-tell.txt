Show what you built or tried with WPM

Category: Show and tell

Show and tell is for sharing what you have made with or around Workforce Planning Management (WPM): a deployment experiment, an integration, a tutorial, a translation, or a screenshot of the demo. Here is a template, then an example of my own.

## Template

- **What it is**, in one sentence, with a link.
- **Why you made it**, in two or three sentences.
- **Something to look at:** a screenshot, a short code sample, or a demo link.
- **How to try it**, in a few steps.
- **One thing that was hard or surprising.**
- **What you'd like back:** feedback, help, or other people's examples.

## Example: the path to production page

**What it is.** A page on the WPM site that says what production use would mean and lists what stands between WPM and that use, each with a readiness estimate: https://workforce-planning-management.github.io/en-us/path-to-production/

**Why I made it.** The project kept saying "not a production system" without saying what was missing. Now each item says what its estimate rests on and what remains, so a reader can judge for themselves.

**Something to look at.** Each item has a slider, built with the Lily design system, set to my estimate. You can move it to try your own view; nothing is saved.

**How to try it.** Open the page, pick your language from the menu, and move a slider with the mouse or the arrow keys.

**What was surprising.** No item reaches 100%, and the estimates are deliberately not averaged: one low item is enough to keep WPM out of production use.

**What I'd like back.** Tell me which estimate you think is wrong, and why.
