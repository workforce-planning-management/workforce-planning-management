Say hello and tell us what brought you to WPM

Category: General

Welcome to Discussions for Workforce Planning Management (WPM). General is for conversation that fits nowhere else: introductions, use cases, and opinions about the project.

I'm Joel, the maintainer. I started WPM to show how an HR platform can handle the whole employee lifecycle, from hire to retire, with privacy built in: what must not be stored gets no column, and an unknown is never shown as a zero. It is demo software with synthetic data only, and the path to production page says how far it is from real use: https://workforce-planning-management.github.io/en-us/path-to-production/

**One question for you:** what brought you to WPM? A sentence or two is plenty, whether you work in HR, build software, or are just curious.
